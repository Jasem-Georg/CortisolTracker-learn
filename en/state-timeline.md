CortisolTracker is not a medical device. The strip gathers events that are already recorded. It does not issue a conclusion and does not suggest a new regimen.

## Analytics of how you feel and of doses taken

The section is called В«State stripВ». Lanes sit side by side: doses, factors, wellbeing, drowsiness, capacity, and notes. You can see in which hour there was a tablet, a factor, and your own mark, without mixing that mark into the concentration calculation.

The strip window has several scales: Day, 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or Year. The axis can run by time or by events. In the events mode, empty gaps collapse: a sparse history fills the width, a dense one scrolls. вЂњHow long afterвЂќ in this mode is read only from the time of the event, not from the width of the step.

A click on an event opens that day on the dose chart. The cortisol curve itself stays on the daily В«Cortisol profileВ» chart. On a window wider than a week, the strip does not draw it.
