CortisolTracker is not a medical device and does not replace a conversation with your endocrinologist. The settings below do not prescribe a dose. They are there so the calculated curve rests on your data, not only on the defaults.

## The idea of the first settings

Without an account you can still build a chart, but the records stay in this browser and are not synced to the server.

For the day to be calculated, and for the charts to be built from you, some first settings are required. First: User profile says who is signed in: name, email, date of birth, biological sex. The medical profile says what an ordinary day looks like: wake time, sleep, weight, and standing medications that affect the calculation.

The order is: registration, user profile, medical profile. Next is [an ordinary day](/learn/en/ordinary-day/): the first dose and the factors. You do not need to change the regimen because of these fields.

## Registration

An account needs a name, an email, and a password. After you sign in, doses, factors, and the profile are stored in the account and can be opened from another device.

Confirming the email is optional at this stage: you can use the app without it.

This is an early check. The app itself warns that, during development, an account may be deleted and a calculation may turn out inaccurate.

## User profile

Here you can change the name, email, and password. Separately, the date of birth and biological sex.

Two switches decide whether those details enter the calculation: «Use date of birth to calculate age» and «Use this sex in the medical profile». If they are on, you do not enter age and sex a second time in the medical profile.

The picture is one example. The labels are English.

## Medical profile

The panel is called «User medical profile» and sits on the main desk under «Doses today» and «Influencing factors today», in «Dose calculation» mode. For an ordinary day, the usual wake time, the hours of sleep, and the weight matter. Wake time is the anchor of the reference curve: the morning rise on the chart is tied to it, not to a fixed eight in the morning.

Age and sex are filled in from the user profile when the matching switches are on.

Standing medications that affect the curve, or the hints next to lab results, are added in this same profile. This is not the list of today's hydrocortisone doses.

![Medical profile: age, weight, sleep, wake time, and standing medications](/learn/images/medical-profile-panel.png)

The picture is one example, **NOT** a regimen to copy. The labels are English.

After registration the app may offer to open this profile. You can fill it in later. An empty profile does not block the first dose, but the reference and the calculation then rest on the defaults.
