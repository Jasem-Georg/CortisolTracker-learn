CortisolTracker is not a medical device. The day's records show a calculation from what you marked. They do not prescribe a regimen and do not replace sick-day rules.

## The first dose

The panel is called В«Add doseВ». A new record is В«Add doseВ».

In the form you set the time, the medication, and the amount. Then press В«Add doseВ» when you are planning. If you press В«Add and takeВ», the time and the amount go straight into the log as taken.

![Add dose form: time, hydrocortisone, amount, and the Add dose and Add and take buttons](/learn/images/add-dose.png)

The picture is one example, **NOT** a regimen to copy. The labels are English.

In the extra panel В«More optionsВ» you can mark whether this dose was taken В«FastingВ» or with food. Food taken at the same time as the corticosteroid shifts the concentration peak in time. Hydrocortisone is available to choose at once. The other medications on the list come after registration.

Every planned and taken dose is shown on the В«Doses todayВ» panel. The chart shows only doses taken in the past and doses planned for the future. If a dose was planned and never taken, once its time has passed it is ignored and no longer affects the chart.

![Doses today: taken hydrocortisone doses and one missed dose](/learn/images/doses-today.png)

The picture is one example, **NOT** a regimen to copy. The labels are English.

A dose can stay В«PlannedВ» until you mark В«Confirm doseВ» on В«Doses todayВ». The chart shows the calculated curve from the logged intakes, not a laboratory result.

## A plan of doses for the day

Several intakes in one day are several records on В«Doses todayВ», each with its own time and amount. В«Recurring doseВ» repeats that record every day, so the ordinary routine does not have to be typed again.

В«GC calcВ» can fill in a time and an amount from the current curve and save them as a planned dose. That is a hint from records already entered. Taking it or changing it is your decision, together with your doctor.

## The first influencing factor

Factors are available after you sign in. The panel is called В«Add factorВ». You need a type, an intensity, and a time.

![Add factor form: time, caffeine, intensity, and duration](/learn/images/add-factor.png)

The picture is one example. The labels are English.

For a first mark, coffee is convenient: the cup and the hour you drank it. The curve is recalculated with that mark.

## Overview of influencing factors

The list has several groups. Drinks and substances: coffee, teas, matcha, L-theanine, alcohol, nicotine. Separately, acute and chronic stress, and physical activity. Food as a factor is about the meal itself. Grapefruit juice, and charcoal or cholestyramine, are marked because they can change how corticosteroids are absorbed.

![Influencing factors today: food, coffee, activity, and alcohol](/learn/images/factors-today.png)

The picture is one example, **NOT** a regimen to copy. The labels are English.

Illness on this list is a cold, COVID-19, or poisoning. Such a mark changes the calculated picture. It does not replace a sick-day plan, an emergency kit, or a doctor's help. In poisoning a tablet may not be absorbed: what to do is decided by your plan with your doctor, not by the app.

Jet lag is a separate type, if the time zones got out of step.

A PPI or an antacid taken with a dose is marked in that dose's form, in the block В«Taken with this doseВ», not as a separate first factor.
