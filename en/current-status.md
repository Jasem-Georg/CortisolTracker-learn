CortisolTracker is not a medical device. The numbers on the panel are a calculation from your records, not a blood or saliva result. Marks of how you feel do not enter the curve and do not make a diagnosis.

## The Current status panel

The panel is called that. The header shows the time and the calculated concentration at that moment.

Inside are four values, all in nmol/L:

- В«ReferenceВ» is the ideal profile without extra factors.
- В«Reference with factorsВ» is the same profile with the factors you marked.
- В«Modelled concentrationВ» is the curve from your doses and factors.
- В«Deviation from targetВ» is how far the modelled concentration is from the target.

The colour of the modelled concentration is the same as on the chart: closer to the reference, a moderate deviation, or stronger. The panel does not suggest changing the regimen.

## Entering how you feel

Below, in the same block, is the form В«How you feel and notesВ». It is there so you can record how you felt in that hour. The line on the form is direct: this does not enter the curve calculation. Without an account, the marks stay on this device only.

Three scales. В«WellbeingВ» runs from a clear lack to a clear excess, and the middle is В«As usualВ». В«DrowsinessВ» runs from a clear head to a state where it is hard to stay awake. В«Capacity to functionВ» runs from an ordinary load to a day when you need help even with daily tasks. Each step in the app has a short caption.

Next to them is the В«NoteВ» field: why this dose was taken, or what else you noticed. If a dose was taken in the last 6 hours, the note can be attached to it. В«Save nowВ» stores the mark in the В«TodayВ» list. You can delete it from that same list.

![How you feel and notes: wellbeing, drowsiness, capacity to function, and a note](/learn/images/current-status-form.png)

The picture is one example, **NOT** a regimen to copy. The labels are English.

These marks do not move the curve and do not replace a conversation with your doctor about how you feel.
